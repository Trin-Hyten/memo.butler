Privacy Policy

Effective 24 July 2026

Memo Butler is a private keepsake diary, built around a single principle: your memories are yours, and they never leave your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Memo Butler does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on any server. We have no servers. We require no account, no sign-up, no email, and no login.

Where your content lives

Everything you write, teach, photograph, or record in Memo Butler — diary entries, the people and things you teach the butler to remember, annotations, and any images — is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is never uploaded, synced to us, or shared with any third party. If you delete the app, that content is removed with it.

No tracking, no analytics, no advertising

Memo Butler contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and makes no network calls to us for any purpose. It works fully offline.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime membership are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us only anonymous, aggregate purchase information through App Store Connect. Your use of the App Store is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera and photos

If you choose to add a photo to a memory, the app accesses your camera or photo library only at that moment, and only with your permission. The resulting image is stored on your device as part of your diary. It is never sent anywhere.

Children

Memo Butler is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@memobutler.com.
